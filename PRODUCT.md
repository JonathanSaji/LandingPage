# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Students and young professionals (18–30) who are tech-savvy and aesthetics-first. They find the page through curiosity or a recommendation. They're evaluating whether SubSync feels worth their time in the first 10 seconds. Speed of comprehension and visual quality are both credibility signals. They have high standards for UI — they use Notion, Linear, Raycast — but they're bored of that aesthetic.

## Product Purpose

SubSync is a constellation of seven specialized apps (TrackerSync, TravelSync, BrainSync, SeatSync, PhotoSync, FluencySync, SteadySync) orbiting a shared intelligence layer called SyncBot. The landing page's job is to make that ecosystem feel like a living, premium, unified product — not a feature list. Success is a visitor who understands the concept immediately and wants to sign up before scrolling to the bottom.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, alive, premium. Active Theory depth + Zenly warmth + Dice.fm energy + Cowboy product clarity.

Three words: **constellation, pulse, craft**.

Tone: confident and specific. Never hype. Never aphoristic. The copy names what each app literally does; the visuals do the emotional work.

## Anti-references

- **AI-generated default**: cream/sand backgrounds, uppercase eyebrow over every section, gradient text, hero-metrics grid (big number + small label + dividers). These patterns signal low craft to the target audience.
- **Overloaded gaming UI**: excess neon, particle storms, RGB overflow. The page can glow and pulse — but every light source must earn its presence. One uncontrolled glow reads as gaming aesthetic; controlled glow reads as premium dark UI.

## Design Principles

1. **Honey is the spine.** The amber/honey accent is the brand's identity and connective tissue. Individual app accent colors are guests — they appear inside product cards and in focused contexts, never competing with the brand layer.
2. **Cinematic depth over surface decoration.** Motion and light carry the emotional register. Copy is specific and brief. Animations reveal rather than distract.
3. **Show the ecosystem in motion.** The 7 apps are not a list — they're a living constellation. The visual system should make that orbital relationship feel real.
4. **Premium through restraint.** Each glow, blur, and animation must be intentional. Earned complexity reads as craft; unearned complexity reads as chaos.
5. **Young and alive, never childish.** The energy comes from pacing, depth, and surprise — not from decoration. The bar is Zenly/Dice.fm, not a student project.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. `prefers-reduced-motion` respected throughout — all animated components should have reduced-motion alternatives (crossfade or instant). Decorative elements marked `aria-hidden`. Semantic HTML landmarks. Focus rings visible (ring-honey/50 pattern established in codebase).
